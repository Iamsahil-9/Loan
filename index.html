FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is the maximum loan amount I can get?
You can borrow up to ₹5,00,000. The final approved amount depends on your income, credit profile, and the partner lender's policy.
What documents do I need to apply?
Only your PAN card and Aadhaar card are required for most loans. Some lenders may ask for 3 months' bank statements for higher amounts.
How long does approval take?
Our system checks your eligibility instantly. Once matched with a lender, approval can happen within 10 minutes during business hours.
What is the interest rate?
Rates vary by lender and your credit profile — typically between 10% and 36% p.a. We always show you the best available rate before you commit.
Will applying affect my CIBIL score?
Checking eligibility on QuickLoan does NOT affect your credit score. A hard inquiry only happens if you formally accept a loan offer.
Is QuickLoan a lender?
No. QuickLoan is a loan marketplace. We connect you with RBI-registered lending partners who provide the actual loan. We do not lend money directly.
